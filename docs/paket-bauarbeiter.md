## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
A night guard on an empty building site hears his own lucky knocking rhythm answered from a pipe that isn't on the plans – it's another Milo on a site below, and when the concrete seals the pipe, the three lucky knocks on his hard hat come from above.  
**Class: story/horror** → Ton angespannt-flüsternd, ruhiger Aufbau, Spannung langsamer und leiser, kurze Hoffnung, Wendung lauter, Ende flach; Stimmung kalt-nächtlich, unheimlich ohne Gewalt.
