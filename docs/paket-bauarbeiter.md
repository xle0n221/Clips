## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
A night guard hears knocking from an iron pipe under his building site, finds out the knocker is another version of himself on a mirrored site below – and when the concrete seals the pipe, the next three knocks come from above.  
**Class: story/horror** → Ton angespannt-flüsternd, ruhiger Aufbau, Spannung langsamer und leiser, Twist lauter und abgehackt, Ende flach; Stimmung kalt-nächtlich, unheimlich ohne Gewalt.

## 2. Title (max 8 words) and 3 alternative hooks
**Title:** Something Under the Site Knocked Back (6 words)

1. „At two in the morning, the ground knocked. Three times. From below.“ *(used)*
2. „There's a pipe on my site that isn't on the plans. It knocks.“
3. „He dropped a tape measure into the ground. It came back with a note.“
