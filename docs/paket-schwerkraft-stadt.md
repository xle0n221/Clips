## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
In this city gravity pulls toward the heaviest person, so everyone fights to stay light and every dropped thing lands on Big Marta – until the lightest kid, Pip, floats away and only Marta, by making herself even heavier, can pull him back into her arms.
**Class: story / comedy-parable** (absurde Welt-Regel, Kurioses) → Ton verschmitzt-ironisch und staunend, ab der Gefahr leise-gespannt, Wende getragen-laut, Ende warm und ruhig.
