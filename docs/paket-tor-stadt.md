## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
In Yesbury the gate only opens when all 1,000 people say yes at the exact same second – for 83 years one voice has said no (the gatekeeper, who would lose his job), until Pip asks the one question everyone agrees on: „Who wants this meeting to be over?“ The gate opens for exactly one second, she gets out – and now it needs a thousand yeses from 999 people.
**Class: comedy (soziale Satire)** → Ton trocken-komisch, Erzähler deadpan (das Absurde wird ernst berichtet), Komik aus Regel-Logik und Untertreibung; Spannung über drei gescheiterte Pläne, Twist laut, letzte Pointe flach und tief.
