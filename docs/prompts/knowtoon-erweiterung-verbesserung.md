# EXTENSION – CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT (Nutzer 14.11.2026; an den Basis-Prompt `knowtoon-videopaket.md` anhängen – diese Regeln gehen bei Widerspruch vor)

```
PREVIOUS VIDEO (update after every upload)
Title: [ZEIT-WELT]
Length / cuts: [103 s, 24 cuts, longest shots 13.6 s, 11.9 s, 11.8 s]
Critique:
- Hook: interesting, but not curious enough
- Storytelling: links between scenes not instantly clear
- Pace: some scenes too long
- On-screen text: single words do not carry the plot
- Drama: no strong emotional peak
- Ending: final word/image needs a much stronger resolution
Stats (if known): [VIEWS | AVG WATCH TIME | % WATCHED TO END | DROP-OFF SECOND]

LESSONS LOG (never delete, only add)
[1. ...]

TASK
Before writing, turn every critique point above into one concrete, measurable fix for the new video and state it in a table: weakness | fix | how it is measured. The new video must beat the previous one on every point, not just avoid the mistake.

UPGRADE RULES
1. Hook: write 5 hooks (not 3). Each must show a result, threat or contradiction before its cause. Reject any hook that could open a different video. The hook question must be stated or shown within 2 s.
2. Scene links: add a column "why this scene follows" (because / but / so). If the link needs more than 8 words to explain, rewrite the scene.
3. Pace: no shot longer than 4 s, except one deliberate hold at the emotional peak (max 6 s). If the previous video had shots over 6 s, cut the average shot length by at least 20%.
4. On-screen text: captions as 2-5 word phrases. Every scene gets one phrase that states what happens, not just a keyword. Test: reading only the captions top to bottom must tell the full story.
5. Emotional peak: place it at 70-80% of the runtime, name the emotion, and build it with three steps: the viewer cares (stakes), hope rises, then the turn. Mark the silence before it.
6. Ending: plant the final word or image in the first third, repeat it once in the middle, and let the last scene give it a new meaning. The ending must answer the hook question in one sentence a viewer could retell.

SCORECARD (output after the package)
Rate hook, scene logic, pace, on-screen text, emotional peak, ending from 1-10, each with one sentence of evidence from your own script. Any score below 9: revise that part and rate again before delivering.

HANDOVER
End with: (a) 3 new lines for the LESSONS LOG, (b) the filled PREVIOUS VIDEO block for this video, ready to paste into the next run, (c) the one thing to test in the next video.
```

## Umsetzung (Orchestrator, 14.11.2026)
- Gilt für jedes Video ab „Bauarbeiter“. Der PREVIOUS-VIDEO-Block und das LESSONS LOG stehen in `docs/lessons-log.md` und werden nach jedem Video fortgeschrieben (nie löschen, nur anfügen).
- Untertitel: Regel 4 ersetzt „ein Wort zur Zeit“ → **2–5-Wort-Phrasen**, gelb mit schwarzem Rand, unteres Drittel; zusätzlich je Szene eine Handlungs-Phrase (On-Screen-Text).
- Schnitt: keine Einstellung > 4 s, außer einem Halt am emotionalen Höhepunkt (≤ 6 s); Ø-Einstellungslänge ≥ 20 % kürzer als im Vorgängervideo.
- Paket bekommt zusätzlich: Schwächen-Tabelle, 5 Hooks, Spalte „why this scene follows“, Scorecard (alle ≥ 9) und Handover.
