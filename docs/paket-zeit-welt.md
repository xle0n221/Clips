## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
In Minuteburg everyone pays with golden time-sand from a jar on their belt – a girl tries to buy her grandfather one more day and finds out an empty jar never killed anyone; the fear of it just keeps the city working.  
**Class: story/mystery** (dystopian parable) → tone dark and tense, calm setup, slower and quieter tension, louder twist; mood dark-cinematic.

## 2. Title (max 8 words) and 3 alternative hooks
**Title:** In This City, You Pay With Time (7 words)

1. „Here, a loaf of bread costs twelve minutes. Of your life.“ *(used)*
2. „My grandpa had one day left. Then he turned his jar over.“
3. „Everyone here is afraid of an empty jar. Nobody checks why.“
