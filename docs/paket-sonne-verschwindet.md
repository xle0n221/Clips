## 1. One-line concept and the class you chose
The Sun vanishes at noon – and for 8 minutes 19 seconds a girl on a beach and her grandpa at his telescope on Earth's night side notice nothing, because light *and* gravity are still on their way; then the sky goes black, Earth flies off straight, and Saturn keeps shining with the vanished Sun's light for two more hours.
**Class: explainer/knowledge (What-if)** → Erklärung als Geschichte mit Figur und Bedrohung (Countdown), Ton staunend mit leiser Spannung (dramatische Ironie: wir wissen es, Juno nicht), Twist laut, Ende ruhig.
